# examples/

Example workflow inputs that produce different looks of the `chibi_humanoid` generator
(enter them on the **Run workflow** form):

| Look | skin | hair | shirt | shorts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forge Kid (default) | `#F2C9A0` | `#3B2417` | `#E8553D` | `#2F4A8A` |
| Deep tone, green kit | `#8D5524` | `#111111` | `#2E9E5B` | `#F0E6D2` |
| Blonde, purple kit | `#FAD7BD` | `#E8C25A` | `#7A4CC2` | `#2B2B2B` |

All variants keep the mandatory T-pose.
